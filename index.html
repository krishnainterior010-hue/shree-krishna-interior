Interior Designer FAQs

Here are some frequently asked questions about our interior design services

What Material Do You Generally Use?

We use ISI-marked BWP/BWR grade plywood (CenturyPly, Greenply), branded laminates (Merino, Greenlam), and soft-close hardware from Hafele, Hettich, and Ebco.

What Are Your General Timelines?

A standard 2BHK or 3BHK turnkey interior project typically takes 45 to 60 working days after design approval.

Do You Do Turnkey Projects?

Yes! We handle complete turnkey execution including civil changes, electrical, false ceiling, furniture, painting, and final decor.

How Do I Get a 3D Visualization?

Book a free consultation with our team at our Chandkheda studio or on-site, and we will prepare customized 2D layouts and 3D concepts for your home.

Do you handle Residential & Commercial Projects?

Yes, we design apartments, bungalows, villas, offices, and retail showrooms across Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar.

Why Should I Hire an Interior Designer?

An experienced designer maximizes space utilization, prevents costly material mistakes, and gives your home a cohesive, luxury finish within your budget.
